# Product: Green Mountain Renovations website

## Platform
web · static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (index.html plus an img/ folder), hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users
- Homeowners in "Kingston and the surrounding area" (the business's own words; the state is not published, so the site never names one) who need drywall hung, taped or finished, usually as part of a renovation.
- They arrive from Facebook (group posts, the owner's page), on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- The owner (Facebook page "GMRDrywall") is the first audience: the site is handed to them free. Owner name and gender unknown; copy avoids both.

## Purpose
1. Show the owner their business done better than he imagined, within one phone screen.
2. Turn a visitor into a free-estimate request, sent through the only channel the business publishes: Facebook Messenger.

## Positioning
A one-person (inferred from "message me") drywall and renovation craftsman whose pitch is finish quality: "hung, taped, finished, and built to last." Logo tagline: "Where Quality Matters."

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: Facebook Messenger only (`https://m.me/61594566614771`, checked: redirects to a Messenger thread). No phone, no email, no address published.
- Estimate form composes a message the visitor can copy and paste into Messenger (Messenger links can't pre-fill text for this page). Copy may be blocked in the in-app browser, so the message stays visible and selectable.
- No prices, reviews, licensing, insurance, hours or years in business: none stated, none claimed.

## Brand commitments
- Logo (from the Facebook profile image): circular wordmark "GREEN MOUNTAIN RENOVATIONS" in deep green around a green-and-gold house mark with a gold taping knife; tagline "WHERE QUALITY MATTERS" in gold serif caps.
- Colors sampled from the logo: green #0F402D, gold #DEAC39.
- Voice: casual craftsman, first person ("message me"), proud of the work.

## Evidence on hand
- Logo (Facebook og:image, 720px).
- One 3-second portfolio clip from the Facebook group post (720x1280): a room mid-finish, seams taped and mudded. The post claims twelve jobs; the downloadable video shows one.
- Post caption, verbatim: "Which ones your favourite?🛠️ Twelve jobs from the portfolio- hung, taped, finished, and built to last. Green Mountain Renovations, serving Kingston and the surrounding area. message me for a free estimate!"

## Principles
- Phone first; Facebook in-app browser is the real environment.
- Only facts from the brief and the owner's own posts. No invented state, prices, reviews or credentials.
- Not a demo: a real site, ready to hand over.
